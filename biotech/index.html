Sponsor meetings with biotechs at the moment they choose a partner.

For CDMOs, CROs, CRDMOs and specialty labs focused on early-phase work. We find biotechs that have just posted a Phase 1 or Phase 1/2 trial with no manufacturing partner named, contact the people who choose partners on your behalf, and book the meetings.

Why now

A first-in-human trial and a fresh financing round are when a small sponsor lines up CMC and clinical partners for the next phase. Both are public: trials on ClinicalTrials.gov, and private raises in SEC Form D filings. We track them by modality, so you hear about the sponsors that match your platform.

What a sample looks like

Before any pilot, we build a free sample for your modality: 10-12 US, UK or EU biotechs that first posted an early-phase trial in the last 30-60 days with no CDMO named, plus their latest financing where we can find it.

The 30-day pilot

  • We build the target list with you, write and send outreach introducing your firm, handle the replies, book meetings into your calendar, and report weekly, including what happened after each meeting (proposal sent, project won).
  • You join a 30-minute kickoff, approve the messaging, and take the meetings.
  • The target is 3 or more qualified sponsor meetings: someone who chooses, or helps choose, the partner for your service at a biotech on the list we agree at kickoff, such as the head of CMC, technical operations or clinical operations. If fewer than 2 arrive, the next month is free.

Who it's for

CDMOs, CROs, CRDMOs and specialty labs with 20-500 staff, a clear modality focus and early-phase capacity, in the US, the UK or India.
