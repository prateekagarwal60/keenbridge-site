Questions

Common questions

Anything else, email us.

Where does the data come from?

Trial registrations on ClinicalTrials.gov, and SEC Form D filings for private raises. We track sponsors by modality, leave out big pharma and CROs, and check every modality against the trial record before a row reaches you.

Does “no manufacturing partner named” mean the sponsor has none?

Not necessarily. It means the trial registration names none, which is what we check, so the sponsor may still be choosing. Your sample links each trial so you can check it yourself.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A meeting with someone who chooses, or helps choose, the partner for your service at a biotech on the list we agree at kickoff: for example the head of CMC, technical operations or manufacturing for CDMO work, or of clinical operations for CRO work; at small biotechs, the CEO or CSO.

Each company counts once. Existing customers and “not interested” replies don’t count, and a booked call you miss still counts.

We already subscribe to a sponsor database. Why add this?

Then you know the data works. We don’t sell data: we do the outreach and book the meetings, so your team gets conversations, not lists.

What does it cost?

The sample is free, with no strings attached. After that, every engagement is a 30-day pilot with a clear target. Email us and we’ll send the terms.
