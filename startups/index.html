Questions

Common questions

What do I need to have in place?

Nothing yet. If you already use a CRM or a sending tool, I build in those. If you don’t, I set up what you need in your name, or run it on my own tools.

Do I need Clay?

No. If you use Clay, I build in your account. If you don’t, I build your lists with my own tools, so you don’t need to buy it.

When do emails start going out?

Once your new inboxes finish warming, about day 14 to 21. New inboxes need a few weeks of warm-up before they can send safely, so they’re set up first and warm while the rest is built.

Who writes the emails?

I do, for your buyers and your offer. You see them, and the first 100 accounts, before the second half of the fee is due.

What if the build runs late?

If the list, contacts, sequence and CRM sync aren’t built and handed over by day 14 for reasons on my side, you can cancel and get your deposit back.

Can I stop the monthly run?

Yes. It’s month to month with 30 days’ notice, and everything stays in your own accounts.
