Outbound systems for startups hiring their first sellers

Keenbridge builds the outbound system your sellers need, then runs it with you if you want: the account list, the right contacts, the emails, and sending that lands in inboxes.

The build: 2 weeks, $3,000 fixed

  • An account list from two public signals that fit your market.
  • The right person at each account, found and checked.
  • A 3-step sequence written for your buyers.
  • Sending domains and inboxes set up and warming, in your own accounts.
  • The sync to your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce or Attio).
  • A written runbook and a recorded walk-through.

Then, if you want it, I run it: $3,000 a month, month to month with 30 days' notice: a new signal or segment each month, the list refreshed weekly, inbox-placement checks, copy tests and a weekly written report.

How you pay: half the build before work starts; the second half once you've seen the first 100 accounts and the emails. If the list, contacts, sequence and CRM sync aren't built and handed over by day 14 for reasons on my side, you can cancel and get the deposit back. (Sending starts when your new inboxes finish warming, about day 14-21.)

Your tools stay yours, billed to you: your own Clay account, domains and inboxes. Syncing Clay to your CRM needs Clay's Growth plan (from $446 a month billed annually, or $495 monthly, paid by you to Clay), unless your sending tool already syncs to your CRM.

Who I am: Keenbridge is a new one-person firm in Bengaluru, India. I'm its founder: an engineer (backend, scraping, data pipelines, LLM extraction) and a former B2B SaaS product manager. I don't have case studies yet, and I won't pretend to.

A free first step: ask for a one-page outline for your company: two signals, who to contact and the first three emails. It's yours to keep.

Keenbridge also offers pay-per-meeting outreach to contract manufacturers and biotech service firms. For startups, I build the system and you own it.
