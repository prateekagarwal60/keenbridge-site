I find companies at the moment they're choosing a new supplier, and book the meetings.

Keenbridge is a small, engineer-led outbound agency. We build data pipelines that notice when a company starts looking for a partner, then we contact the right person on our client's behalf and book a qualified meeting.

How it works

  1. Spot the moment. Public records show when a company's needs change, often weeks before it starts asking around.
  2. Reach the right person. We write to the people who choose suppliers, introducing our client, one plain email at a time.
  3. Book the meeting. We handle the replies and put qualified calls on our client's calendar, then report weekly on what happened after each one.

Every engagement starts with a free sample built for your business, then a 30-day pilot with a clear target.
