How it works

From public record to booked meeting

The same three steps, whichever segment you’re in.

  1. Spot the change

    Public records show when a company’s needs change, often weeks before it starts asking around.

  2. Reach the right person

    We write to the people who choose suppliers, introducing our client, one plain email at a time.

  3. Book the meeting

    We handle the replies and put qualified calls on our client’s calendar, then report weekly on what happened after each one.
