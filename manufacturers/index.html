RFQ calls with OEMs that already buy parts like yours, from overseas.

For US job shops in CNC machining, sheet metal fabrication and injection molding. We find OEMs that import the kind of parts you make, contact their sourcing teams on your behalf, and book the calls.

Why now

This year's tariff changes have many OEMs pricing a move of overseas work back to the US. US customs records show which companies import which parts, from where, and how often. Those are the buyers most likely to send a US shop an RFQ this year.

The 30-day pilot

  • We build the target list with you, write and send outreach introducing your shop, handle the replies, book calls into your calendar, and report weekly, including what happened after each call.
  • You join a 30-minute kickoff, approve the messaging, and take the calls.
  • The target is 4 or more qualified conversations or RFQs: someone who buys or specifies parts at an OEM on the list we agree at kickoff. If fewer than 2 arrive, the next month is free.

Who it's for

US shops with 20-500 staff that make parts to customer drawings, with ISO 9001 or better. AS9100, ISO 13485 and IATF 16949 shops are a particularly good fit.
