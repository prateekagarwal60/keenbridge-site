The buying signal

Find the OEMs that import parts like yours

This year’s tariff changes give many OEMs good reason to price a move of overseas work back to the US. US customs records show which companies import which parts, from where, and how often. Those are the buyers most likely to send a US shop an RFQ this year.

Aerospace or defense shop?

Aerospace and defense parts are rarely imported from China, so for these shops we use a different signal: recent Defense Department contract awards, published in the Defense Department's daily contract announcements, to primes and Tier 1 suppliers in or near your region, for the kinds of parts you make.

Who imports
OEMs and tier-1 suppliers. We drop distributors, freight forwarders and consumer brands.
What they import
The parts as declared, matched to your capability: machined, fabricated or molded.
How recently
The month of the latest shipment, within the last 12 months.

Questions

Common questions

Anything else, email us.

Where do the rows come from?

US customs records. When goods arrive in the US by sea, the bill-of-lading records show which company imported which parts, from where, and when. We keep OEMs and tier-1 suppliers, drop distributors, forwarders and consumer brands, and check every row against its record.

For aerospace and defense shops we use recent Defense Department contract awards, published in the Defense Department's daily contract announcements, instead.

What counts as a qualified conversation?

A call with someone who buys or specifies parts (sourcing, procurement, supply chain, or manufacturing or supplier-quality engineering) at an OEM on the list we agree at kickoff, or an RFQ from such an OEM.

Each company counts once. Existing customers and “not interested” replies don’t count, and a booked call you miss still counts.

Who sends the emails?

We do, from our own inboxes, introducing your shop. You approve the messaging first; we handle the replies and book the calls into your calendar.

How is this different from reps or RFQ marketplaces?

Reps sell into accounts they already know in their territory. We find OEMs you don’t know yet, nationally, while they’re re-sourcing, and hand the meetings to you or your reps.

On marketplaces, many shops bid on the same RFQ, often on price. We open direct conversations with the people who buy or specify parts.

What does it cost?

The sample is free, with no strings attached. After that, every engagement is a 30-day pilot with a clear target. Email us and we’ll send the terms.
